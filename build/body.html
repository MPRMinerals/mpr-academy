The company

Whoever does not control the chain controls nothing.

The founder entered copper in 2020 as a broker, and learned that lesson the only way it is ever learned. In 2022 the company was rebuilt from the ground up as a vertically integrated producer.

Today MPR buys ore under offtake contracts with licensed operators in Likasi and Kolwezi, processes it through SX-EW hydrometallurgical plants under tolling agreements, and moves the finished cathode into a bonded warehouse in Ndola, Zambia, where title passes to the buyer.

The chain

Five steps, and we own every one.

01

Ore procurement

Offtake contracts with licensed mining operators in Likasi and Kolwezi, in Haut Katanga and Lualaba. Grades are verified by XRF scanning before loading.

02

Transport to plant

Controlled haulage from the mine site to the plant, with weighbridge and sampling at reception.

03

SX-EW processing

Solvent extraction and electrowinning under tolling agreement. The output is copper cathode at 99.97 to 99.99 percent Cu.

04

Inspection and logistics

Independent inspection of quality and weight, then bonded transport to Ndola, Zambia.

05

Delivery and payment

Release FCA Ndola against documents. Payment under the letter of credit. The performance bond is released once the buyer confirms receipt.

Inside the cell

Copper leaves the solution and becomes a plate.

01

Anodes and cathodes alternate in the tank, hung from a busbar that carries the current.

02

Direct current pulls copper ions out of the electrolyte and deposits them on the cathode face.

03

Oxygen releases at the anode and rises through the solution as fine bubbles.

04

After several days the plate is lifted, washed, weighed and strapped into a bundle.

Commercial terms

The same terms for every buyer.

Every market, every counterparty, every contract. The structure does not change with the size of the buyer.

Settlement

Letter of credit at sight

Irrevocable documentary letter of credit, MT700, at sight, subject to UCP 600, issued or confirmed by a top tier bank. No prepayment and no transferable instrument.

Banking

Settle where you bank

MPR holds banking in the United Arab Emirates and in the United States. A North American buyer can have the instrument advised or confirmed through a United States bank and settle domestically, without routing across several jurisdictions.

Security

Performance bond

MPR issues a 2 percent performance bond covering quality, quantity and delivery. It is released once the buyer confirms receipt of the goods.

Production

Produce to contract

Production starts when the instrument is operative. There is no speculative stock and no allocation promise.

Delivery basis

FCA Ndola, Zambia

Delivery into a leading international bonded warehouse in Ndola, under Incoterms 2020. This is the standard basis and the only basis offered by default.

Beyond Ndola

Dar es Salaam or Durban

Where a buyer requires delivery at a further port, MPR requires an advance payment representing an agreed percentage of the contract face value, covering the transit period and the risk carried while the cargo travels. The percentage is fixed contract by contract.

Roadmap

Own ore, then our own plant.

Phase 1Fourth quarter 2026

Exploitation begins on the 104 mining squares at Kambove, where MPR is owner and partner. Own ore replaces third party supply at the head of the chain.

Phase 2From early 2027

Construction of an MPR processing plant on the Kambove concession, designed for 5,000 MT of copper cathode per month and 60,000 MT of cobalt per year.

Phase 3Long term

Refining capacity in Europe, to serve industrial consumers closer to their own plants.

Until the Kambove plant is running, MPR produces through its operations in Likasi and Kolwezi, and available tonnage is confirmed case by case against a signed contract.
