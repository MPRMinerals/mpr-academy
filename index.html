The process

SX-EW: copper without a furnace.

Solvent Extraction — Electrowinning is a hydrometallurgical route: liquids and electric current instead of heat. It produces cathode-grade copper directly from oxide ore, on site, in a matter of days.

Stage 01

Leaching

Crushed ore is stacked on lined pads and irrigated with dilute sulfuric acid (H₂SO₄). The acid dissolves copper out of the rock, producing the Pregnant Leach Solution — the PLS.

Crushed ore + H₂SO₄→ Pregnant Leach Solution
Stage 02

Solvent extraction

The PLS is mixed with an organic solvent that captures copper ions and rejects impurities. Stripping with fresh acid then yields a clean, concentrated copper electrolyte.

PLS + organic solvent→ Loaded organic→ Pure electrolyte
Stage 03

Electrowinning

A direct current plates copper out of the electrolyte onto stainless-steel blanks. After five to seven days the plates are harvested, washed, weighed and strapped into bundles.

Electrolyte + current→ 5–7 days plating→ Grade A cathode ✓

No smelting means no sulfur dioxide and a lower energy intensity than the pyrometallurgical route — plus access to oxide ore bodies a furnace simply cannot treat.

Trade terms

FCA bonded warehouse. LC at sight.

Two clauses carry most of the risk in a copper trade: where the goods change hands, and how the money moves. We keep both neutral, inspected and bankable.

FCA

FCA — Free Carrier, bonded warehouse

We deliver the cathodes into a customs-supervised bonded warehouse and hand them to the buyer’s carrier. Risk transfers at that exact point.

MPR→ Bonded warehouse, LC at sight→ Buyer
LC

Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight

The buyer’s bank commits to pay against compliant documents, typically within five business days of presentation.

Documents presented under the credit

  • Commercial invoice — quantity, LME-linked unit price, total value
  • Weight & assay certificate — signed by SGS or Bureau Veritas
  • Warehouse receipt — proof of FCA delivery
  • Packing list — bundle count, plate count, net and gross weight
  • Certificate of origin — confirms DRC origin

How a shipment works

  1. Contract signed

    Quantity, quality, LME pricing formula and delivery window are agreed.

  2. Letter of credit issued

    The buyer’s bank issues an irrevocable LC at sight in MPR’s favour; our bank advises and validates it.

  3. Delivery to the bonded warehouse

    Cathodes are moved into the bonded warehouse and handed to the buyer’s carrier — risk transfers under FCA.

  4. Independent inspection

    SGS or Bureau Veritas weighs the bundles and certifies the assay before any payment is triggered.

  5. Documents & payment

    The full document set is presented to the bank, which pays at sight against compliant documents.

Discrepant documents delay payment. That is why every shipment is documented to the letter of the credit before it leaves the warehouse.
