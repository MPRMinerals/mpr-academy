Process

SX-EW. Copper without a furnace.

Solvent extraction and electrowinning: liquids and electric current instead of heat. Cathode-grade copper comes off the plant directly, with no smelting stage.

01 · Leaching

Crushed ore is irrigated with dilute sulfuric acid, which dissolves the copper out of the rock.

Ore + H₂SO₄→ Leach solution
02 · Solvent extraction

An organic solvent captures the copper ions and rejects impurities, yielding a clean, concentrated electrolyte.

Leach solution→ Pure electrolyte
03 · Electrowinning

Current plates the copper onto stainless-steel blanks. The plates are harvested, washed, weighed and strapped into bundles.

Electrolyte + current→ Grade A cathode ✓

No smelting means no sulfur dioxide and a lower energy intensity than the furnace route.

Terms

FCA Ndola. LC at sight.

Two clauses carry the risk in a copper trade: where the goods change hands, and how the money moves. We keep both neutral, inspected and bankable.

FCA

FCA — bonded warehouse, Ndola, Zambia

We deliver the cathodes into a leading international bonded warehouse in Ndola and hand them to your carrier. Risk transfers at that point. Export costs, freight, insurance and destination duties are yours, under ICC Incoterms® 2020.

MPR→ Ndola→ Your carrier
LC

Irrevocable Letter of Credit at sight

Your bank pays against compliant documents, typically within five business days of presentation. No deferred payment: the guarantee sits with a bank, not with a counterparty.

Documents presented
  • Commercial invoice
  • Weight & assay certificate — SGS or Bureau Veritas
  • Warehouse receipt — proof of FCA delivery
  • Packing list — bundle and plate count, net and gross weight
  • Certificate of origin — DR Congo

How a shipment works

  1. Contract

    Quantity, quality, LME pricing formula and delivery window are agreed.

  2. Letter of credit

    Your bank issues an irrevocable LC at sight in MPR’s favour; our bank advises and validates it.

  3. Delivery & inspection

    Cathodes enter the Ndola bonded warehouse. SGS or Bureau Veritas weighs the bundles and certifies the assay.

  4. Documents & payment

    The document set is presented to the bank, which pays at sight against compliant documents.

Every lot is weighed and assayed by an independent inspector before payment is triggered.
